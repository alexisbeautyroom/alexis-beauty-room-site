Please Read Before Booking

Respecting the schedule, for everyone.

By booking, you're agreeing to these policies. Please communicate as needed — we're understanding, just keep us in the loop.

  • —A 50% deposit is due at booking, charged per service — mani and pedi are deposited separately.
  • —A card on file is required to secure your appointment, covering a 50% cancellation or no‑show fee if needed.
  • —To reschedule, give at least 36 hours' notice. Canceling inside 36 hours is treated as a late cancellation.
  • —Running 8–10 minutes late adds a $10 late fee. More than 10 minutes late, and the appointment is canceled — the cancellation fee applies.
  • —At booking, you'll confirm: no fungus, discoloration, or injuries; that nails and toes are healthy; and that you agree to all studio policies.
  • —No‑shows and unpaid fees result in being blocked from future bookings.
